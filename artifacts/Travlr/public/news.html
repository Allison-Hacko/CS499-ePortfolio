Helpful vacation tips

What should I bring?

Pack weather-appropriate clothing, comfortable footwear, sun protection, any required medication, travel documents, and a reusable water bottle.

Plan activities ahead

Check opening hours, local conditions, booking requirements, and transportation before setting your schedule.

Prepare for the water

Check weather and water conditions, use appropriate safety equipment, and follow lifeguard instructions.

Keep a basic first-aid kit

Bring basic first-aid supplies and know how to contact local emergency services at your destination.
