04 / Reflection

Growth, learning, and what comes next

Building these projects helped me understand that software development involves more than getting code to run. I learned to look more closely at how applications are structured, how data is handled, and how design decisions affect reliability, security, and performance.

Each artifact challenged me in a different way, from strengthening authentication and database operations to improving algorithms and organizing a 3D scene. Working through these changes helped me become more deliberate about testing, troubleshooting, and explaining the reasoning behind my decisions.

As I continue developing my skills, I want to build applications that are functional, secure, maintainable, and thoughtfully designed. This portfolio represents both the work I have completed and the foundation I am building for the next stage of my career.
